Help for the Goldstone Properties app (iPhone, Android and gpflips.com)

The Goldstone Properties app is used by the Goldstone Properties team and the contractors who work with us to manage properties, jobs, tasks, photos and messages. Accounts are created by invitation only.

Contact us

Email elihassan16@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you within one business day. Contractors can also message the Goldstone team from the chat inside the app.

Common questions

How do I get an account?

Goldstone Properties creates your login and sends it to you. If you work with us and don’t have one yet, email us.

I forgot my password.

Email us or message your Goldstone contact and we’ll set a new one for you.

I’m not getting notifications.

In the app, tap your initials (top right) → Turn on notifications → Allow. If you tapped “Don’t Allow” before, open your phone’s Settings → Notifications → Goldstone Properties → Allow Notifications.

A photo or video didn’t send.

Uploads keep going in the background and pick up where they left off when you reopen the app. If one shows “Try again”, tap it once you have a connection.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address on your account and we’ll remove your login and personal information within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
